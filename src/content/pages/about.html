About Us

Winnebago Web Designs

Big Business Treatment On A Small Business Budget

Hi! My name is Rudy L. I am the owner and sole developer of Winnebago Web Designs. I got into programming when I was in middle school. I fell in love with the problem-solving that software development offers, and have been pursuing my passion ever since. Over the course of four-and-a-half years I earned a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, and decided to start calling businesses to offer my help.

I've found that many small businesses don't have the funds to spend thousands of dollars on a new website. And those that do often end up with a site that looks like it's 10 years out of date with current design trends. It's a frustrating position to be in, which is why I offer a $0 down, $150 a month model. That way, a small business can handle the cost much easier without breaking the bank.
